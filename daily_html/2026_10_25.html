Grounded Exploration for Open-Ended Image Editing

Open-ended image-editing requests, such as ``decorate this room,'' admit multiple valid realizations. Direct sampling may produce ungrounded variation, whereas explicit diversity prompting often produces superficial differences. We study how to ground these requests without prematurely eliminating their legitimate ambiguity. We introduce a two-stage framework that first identifies diverse, feasible edit scopes and then realizes them as executable edit instructions. Across 300 open-ended image-editing tasks, our approach achieves 24.6% higher mean semantic dispersion and 17.1% higher mean groundedness than direct diversity prompting, while producing more valid and distinct realizations. These results show that structuring feasible edit directions before realization supports more useful grounded exploration.

TLDR: This paper introduces a two-stage framework for open-ended image editing that grounds vague prompts by first identifying feasible edit scopes before generating specific instructions, resulting in higher semantic diversity and groundedness.

TLDR: 本文提出了一种用于开放式图像编辑的两阶段框架，通过在生成具体指令前先识别可行的编辑范围，解决了模糊指令带来的生成结果不接地气或多样性不足的问题。

Mix Forcing: Causal Pre-Training via Mixture Attention for Interactive World Models

While interactive video world models require autoregressive generation, directly adapting pretrained bidirectional video diffusion models into autoregressive ones with teacher forcing can disrupt their visual priors and degrade autoregressive generation quality. In this paper, we introduce Mix Forcing, a causal pre-training paradigm for interactive video world models, implemented with the Mixture-of-Bidirectional-and-Autoregressive (MoBA) attention. Mix Forcing jointly trains a video diffusion model with both teacher forcing and bidirectional training, yielding a strong causal initialization for few-step post-training. In self-attention, Mix Forcing leverages teacher forcing to learn causal next-chunk prediction for autoregressive rollout, while using bidirectional attention to preserve pretrained visual priors. To support online text interaction, Mix Forcing further adopts a chunk-wise cross-attention design that conditions each video chunk on a shared environment prompt and its own local prompt, without exposing future instructions to the current chunk. Following causal pre-training on a 14B image-to-video model, we apply consistency and distribution matching distillation to obtain a four-step autoregressive generator. Experiments on our internal benchmark and public benchmarks, including WBench and SANA-WM-Bench, show that our model achieves state-of-the-art visual quality and timely event following with precise camera control. Notably, our model supports high-fidelity long-horizon generation without quality degradation even over one-hour rollouts. Project page is here: https://zelingao98.github.io/mixforcing

TLDR: Mix Forcing introduces a causal pre-training paradigm using Mixture-of-Bidirectional-and-Autoregressive attention to adapt bidirectional video diffusion models for high-fidelity, long-horizon interactive world modeling.

TLDR: Mix Forcing 提出了一种基于“双向与自回归混合注意力”的因果预训练范式，旨在将双向视频扩散模型转化为高质量、长时序的交互式世界模型。

UltraFlash: Accelerating Megapixel Visual Synthesis

Megapixel visual synthesis with latent diffusion models requires operating far beyond the resolutions at which these models are typically trained. Cascade refinement is a leading strategy for this setting: it starts from a base image at a native resolution and progressively upsamples it to the target resolution through multiple refinement stages. Each stage typically performs a pixel-space resolution transition followed by latent-space denoising. While prior acceleration efforts mainly focus on reducing denoising cost, we identify a complementary and underexplored bottleneck: the repeated movement of representations through the VAE. We call these operations pixel-space transitions. In cascade pipelines, intermediate latents are often decoded to RGB, resized, and re-encoded before refinement, while final latents are decoded patch by patch with dense overlap to suppress boundary artifacts. We propose UltraFlash, a transition-efficient approach that accelerates these VAE-heavy operations. UltraFlash has two components. Latent Hyperloop replaces intermediate RGB round trips with a learned latent-space shortcut that approximates the decode-resize-encode transformation without repeated VAE decoding and encoding. Sparse Patch-based Decoding accelerates final reconstruction by addressing patch seams caused by inconsistent GroupNorm statistics and reusing cached statistics across patches, enabling sparse VAE decoding with much smaller overlap. Integrated into a state-of-the-art cascade baseline, UltraFlash reduces 4K generation latency by approximately 2x while improving image quality. These results show that optimizing pixel-space transitions can improve both efficiency and fidelity, offering an effective route toward fast, high-quality megapixel visual synthesis.

TLDR: UltraFlash accelerates megapixel latent diffusion models by replacing costly VAE-based pixel-space transitions with a latent-space 'Hyperloop' and optimizing patch-based decoding to minimize artifacts and latency.

TLDR: UltraFlash 通过引入潜在空间“超循环（Hyperloop）”替换高昂的 VAE 像素空间转换，并优化分块解码过程，在大幅降低 megapixel 图像合成延迟的同时提升了图像质量。

Distilling Dense Trajectory Teachers For Large-Motion Video World Models

Video world models are expected to generate videos that accurately follow specified camera trajectories while maintaining consistent 3D structures. However, existing models are predominantly trained on densely sampled videos with small inter-frame camera motions. When applied to sparse, large step trajectories, they often produce frozen frames, inaccurate camera motion, and severe geometric drift. We attribute this degradation to a mismatch between the gradual viewpoint transitions seen during training and the large viewpoint changes required at inference time. To address this problem, we propose Dense Trajectory Teacher Distillation (DTTD), a post-training framework that transfers a model's generation capability from dense trajectories to large camera motions. Given a sparse trajectory, DTTD interpolates additional camera poses to construct a dense trajectory covering the same path. The frozen base model then generates reliable teacher videos along this familiar dense trajectory, and pose aligned teacher observations are used to evaluate candidate videos generated under the original sparse trajectory. In this way, the model serves as its own generation teacher, requiring neither additional video data nor a stronger external video generator. We further combine dense teacher supervision with geometry aware rewards that assess cross frame consistency and camera trajectory following, and use the resulting preferences for offline preference optimization. To stably expand the model's motion range, we introduce a cumulative curriculum that progressively incorporates larger motion steps while retaining simpler trajectories to reduce capability forgetting.

TLDR: The paper introduces DTTD, a post-training framework that enables video world models to handle large-motion trajectories by using the model itself as a teacher through dense trajectory distillation and preference optimization.

TLDR: 该论文提出了DTTD训练框架，通过利用模型自身的密集轨迹蒸馏与偏好优化，解决了视频世界模型在处理大位移相机轨迹时出现的生成质量下降与几何漂移问题。

Qwen-Image-PE: Learning Visual Design for Text-to-Image Prompt Enhancement

Learning visual design is essential for prompt enhancement (PE) to turn underspecified user requests into coherent visual specifications for text-to-image (T2I) generation. Existing methods preserve user intent and improve overall visual appeal, with less explicit emphasis on fine-grained design dimensions. To address this gap, we introduce Qwen-Image-PE, which learns visual design through two-stage training. Supervised fine-tuning with Caption-Based Reverse Distillation (CRD) uses dense captions of well-designed images as design supervision. Reinforcement learning with Groupwise Design Reward (GDR) jointly compares all candidate images for the same request to refine lighting, composition, layout, and detail. On Qwen-Image-Bench with Qwen-Image-2.1, Qwen-Image-PE-397B improves over original requests by 7.81 points, while Qwen-Image-PE-9B outperforms all evaluated PE baselines. Further evaluations show gains in visual design, aesthetics, image quality, and human preference. We find that PE scales along three axes: training data, model size, and information density, with sufficient information density as a prerequisite for effective enhancement. Moreover, we demonstrate that Qwen-Image-PE generalizes to four additional T2I backbones, even surpassing native prompt enhancers on HunyuanImage-2.1 and FLUX 3 Image Klein. We release Qwen-Image-PE-9B at https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1-PE-T2I.

TLDR: Qwen-Image-PE introduces a two-stage training approach, using caption-based distillation and groupwise design reinforcement learning, to enhance T2I prompts with a focus on fine-grained visual design principles.

TLDR: Qwen-Image-PE 提出了一种两阶段训练方法，通过基于标题的蒸馏和分组设计奖励机制，旨在通过细粒度的视觉设计维度增强文本到图像 (T2I) 生成的提示词。

World-in-Context: Interactive Video World Modeling with In-Context Conditioning

Interactive video world models require coordinated control over viewing perspectives, character actions, and camera motion. Existing methods typically leave view modes implicit in text captions, conflate action with camera motion, and inject controls through fragmented architectural pathways that capture interdependencies only indirectly. In this work, we present World-in-Context, an interactive world modeling framework powered by In-Context Conditioning (ICC). Rather than relying on specialized conditioning pathways, World-in-Context unifies view, action, and camera controls into explicit tokens contextualized directly within a shared self-attention space. Our formulation reveals that (i) explicit view tokens disentangle conflicting dynamics and unlock emergent view switching from single-view data, while (ii) action and camera inputs play complementary roles in governing local responsiveness and global stability. To preserve temporal continuity across execution intervals, we further introduce Interaction-Transport RoPE (IT-RoPE), parameterizing temporal attention with accumulated control transitions. On WBench, World-in-Context-5B achieves navigation performance on par with 22B EchoWM ($81.5$ vs. $81.6$) and attains a $93.8\%$ perspective-switching success rate, substantially outperforming existing 5B baselines ($18.8\%$). Controlled analyses further demonstrate compounding advantages of World-in-Context under richer conditioning mixtures, favorable data efficiency, and natural applicability to causal rollouts.

TLDR: World-in-Context introduces a unified framework for interactive video world modeling by using In-Context Conditioning to integrate view, action, and camera controls into a shared self-attention space, significantly improving steerability and temporal continuity.

TLDR: World-in-Context 提出了一种通过上下文内调节（In-Context Conditioning）实现交互式视频世界建模的框架，将视角、动作和相机控制统一在共享的注意力空间中，显著提升了控制精度与时间连贯性。

Patch-Derived or Register Tokens? Comparing Variable-Length Image Tokenizers

Image tokenizers convert pixels into compact latent sequences for efficient generative modeling. Most approaches arrange latents on a downsampled two-dimensional grid, tying sequence length to image resolution. We compare two variable-length strategies: computationally efficient patch-derived tokens, which inherit explicit spatial organization, and more demanding register tokens, which have no predefined spatial layout. Our reconstruction results reveal that patch-derived tokens perform substantially better under extreme compression, whereas register tokens achieve slightly better reconstruction quality at intermediate budgets. At high token budgets, both approaches achieve similar reconstruction quality. For downstream generative tasks, register tokens outperform patch-derived tokens. Adding representation alignment with external pretrained visual encoders substantially improves the generative performance of patch-derived tokens, allowing them to match or outperform register tokens depending on the token budget. We further find that both approaches learn spatially localized representations. Patch-derived tokens largely retain their explicitly imposed spatial organization, while register tokens develop an implicit two-dimensional spatial structure despite having no predefined layout. Representation alignment reduces this spatial organizational constraint, enabling individual tokens to exert a stronger non-local influence.

TLDR: This paper evaluates patch-derived versus register-based variable-length image tokenizers, finding that while patch-derived tokens excel at extreme compression, register tokens offer better generative performance, which can be mitigated via representation alignment.

TLDR: 本文对比了基于补丁（patch-derived）和基于寄存器（register tokens）的可变长度图像分词器，发现前者在极高压缩比下表现更佳，而后者在生成任务中更具优势，且通过表示对齐可进一步提升性能。

Semantic-First MixGRPO: Reliable Emotional Image Generation via Temporal Decoupling

Controllable image synthesis should modify visual affect without replacing the objects, layout, or relations specified by a prompt. Existing methods address this tension through conditioning architectures or auxiliary losses, while mixed ordinary differential equation (ODE) and stochastic differential equation (SDE) sampling has mainly been used to accelerate group relative policy optimization (GRPO). We instead study stochastic-window position as an attribute-control variable for flow-based image generation. Using FLUX.1-dev with EmotiCrafter-style valence-arousal conditioning, we compare fixed early, middle, and late four-transition SDE windows, progressive MixGRPO, and full-path Flow-GRPO under matched settings. Because step 0 has the highest noise, our semantic-first schedule uses deterministic ODE sampling at steps 0-19 to establish content, then confines stochastic exploration and policy updates to steps 20-23. Across three seeds, it achieves a CLIPScore of $29.662\pm0.276$, cross-affect DINO consistency of $80.1\pm0.9$, and semantic drift of 2.9%, while reducing training cost by 33.5% relative to full-path Flow-GRPO. Full-path stochastic training remains more accurate on valence-arousal targets, revealing a genuine trade-off. Semantic-first nevertheless improves both affect and semantic metrics over progressive scheduling, preserves the principal subject in 95.98% of images at five extreme or neutral targets, and receives the highest semantic-preservation and overall-preference ratings in a 30-participant study. These results identify probability-flow time as a practical graphics control dimension for reliable affective appearance manipulation.

TLDR: The paper introduces 'Semantic-First MixGRPO', a method for affective image generation using FLUX.1 that decouples content formation (via ODE) from emotional refinement (via SDE/GRPO) to preserve semantic integrity while controlling visual affect.

TLDR: 本文提出了“Semantic-First MixGRPO”方法，通过在FLUX.1采样过程中解耦内容生成（使用ODE）与情感调整（使用SDE/GRPO），在保持图像语义一致性的同时实现了可控的情感表达。

Rethinking Microscopy Generation: Co-Designed Diffusion for Biologically Interpretable Single-Cell Synthesis

Generative models for microscopy can already produce realistic whole-image fields of view, but offer no principled way to control how each individual cell looks. We address this gap by recasting microscopy generation at the single-cell level and designing the input controls and the model's internal representation jointly, so that both are organized along the same biologically meaningful directions. On the input side, we aggregate a long list of standard cell measurements into a small set of semantic axes (size, elongation, brightness, texture, etc.) that are each named, measurable, and traversable. On the model side, we introduce BioVAE, an encoder-decoder whose latent space is explicitly aligned with the same set of measurements. A key finding is that the benefit of biologically structuring the latent emerges only when conditioning is also moved to the semantic axes, showing that the two ends must be designed jointly. Experiments on LIVECell show that semantic-axis conditioning substantially improves generative fidelity over high-dimensional conditioning, a blind study with biologists confirms the realism of generated cells, and per-axis traversals produce stable and interpretable phenotypic changes.

TLDR: The paper introduces BioVAE, a generative framework that co-designs input controls and latent spaces based on biologically meaningful semantic axes to enable interpretable and controllable single-cell microscopy synthesis.

TLDR: 该论文提出了一种名为BioVAE的生成框架，通过协同设计输入控制与隐含空间，使其与具有生物学意义的语义轴对齐，从而实现了可解释且可控的单细胞显微图像生成。

KINESYS: Test-Time Adaptation for Reference-Based Effect Editing in Images

Visual effects are often easier to demonstrate via reference than to describe in words. We study effect-reference editing, where a reference image shows a desired visual effect and language identifies which properties should guide the edit. Existing single-pass editing techniques often fail to make fine-grained edits, resulting in unintended changes to source content or deviations from the reference effect. We introduce KINESYS, a test-time adaptation framework for effect-reference editing that combines reference-aware and source-aware feedback to transfer the desired effect while preserving the composition and content of the original shot. To ensure KINESYS can produce a diverse set of edits that preserve the source, we develop a robust optimization strategy in which the generative model is fine-tuned across multiple noise samples and optimization runs. Evaluation with VLM judges, source preservation metrics (LPIPS, TPIPS), and a user study shows that KINESYS outperforms state-of-the-art image editors in source preservation; on effect transfer, it remains competitive with Nano Banana 2 and outperforms open-weight editors.

TLDR: KINESYS is a test-time adaptation framework for image editing that uses reference images and language prompts to achieve precise effect transfer while maintaining original content integrity through multi-sample robust optimization.

TLDR: KINESYS 是一种用于图像编辑的测试时自适应框架，通过结合参考图像和文本提示，利用多样本稳健优化策略，在实现精确视觉效果迁移的同时保持原始图像的内容完整性。

Support-guided OOD Removal with Diffusion

Many real-world signals are corrupted by localized, structured perturbations whose distribution is unknown: an intermittent background noise in a music recording, a watermark stamped on a photograph, a cast shadow. We study the task of recovering a clean signal $x$ from an observation $y$ containing such a localized out-of-distribution (OOD) corruption. We propose Support-guided OOD Removal with Diffusion, SORD, an iterative algorithm to recover $x$ using only two ingredients: a binary map of the corruption support and a single diffusion model pretrained on clean signals. SORD does not require any paired data or any statistical model of the corruption. We evaluate SORD on natural images and human faces with spatially localized corruptions including handwritten digits, other images, watermarks, and shadows, as well as audio recordings of different musical instruments with temporally localized corruptions including other instruments and human speech. SORD is competitive with, and often outperforms, posterior-sampling inpainting given the same diffusion prior and the same mask, source-separation baselines that require a separate diffusion model for each source, and, on several benchmarks, supervised networks trained on paired clean and corrupted signals. The code is released here: \href{https://github.com/voilalab/SORD}{https://github.com/voilalab/SORD}.

TLDR: SORD is an iterative diffusion-based algorithm that removes localized, unknown OOD corruptions from signals using only a corruption support mask and a pretrained clean-signal diffusion model, outperforming existing supervised and posterior-sampling methods.

TLDR: SORD 提出了一种基于预训练扩散模型的迭代算法，仅需腐蚀区域的二值掩码即可去除图像或音频中未知的局部离群噪声，且无需成对训练数据，性能优于现有的监督学习及后验采样方法。
