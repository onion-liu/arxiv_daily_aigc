FastGRN: Few-Step Generative Refinement Networks

Generative Refinement Networks (GRN) generate an image by repeatedly re-predicting and refining every token of a discrete latent. They reach high fidelity, but need dozens of Markov steps. We therefore train few-step GRNs on the distribution of their own samples. We use its own uniform-state corruption to perform distribution matching on discrete tokens, which lets a discrete generator learn by backpropagation, and we pair noise with samples in the manner of rectified flow. We find that the steps of a few-step GRN do not carry the same amount of information or the same repair load. We therefore exploit its discrete refinement during training and build few-step GRNs in three parts. Oversampling samples the student with more steps than it was trained for and refines its judge on these samples. Non-uniform sampling trains only the last step and hands it most of the information at sampling time. Refinement confidence keeping holds on to the tokens the student is already sure of. We train a fast class-conditional generator on ImageNet 256 and, with the same method, a 2B text-to-image model. On ImageNet 256, the 3-step FastGRN beats the FID of GRN. On a 2B binary-token text-to-image GRN, a 4-step student also nearly matches the GenEval of GRN-T2I 2B.

TLDR: FastGRN introduces a method to reduce the inference steps of Generative Refinement Networks (GRN) by utilizing distribution matching, non-uniform step allocation, and refinement confidence keeping, achieving high-fidelity image generation in only 3-4 steps.

TLDR: FastGRN 提出了一种减少生成细化网络（GRN）推理步数的方法，通过分布匹配、非均匀步长分配和细化置信度保留，实现了仅需 3-4 步的高保真图像生成。

FinVision: Multimodal AI for Visualizing Financial Narratives

Financial narratives contain rich information about firms' performance and business conditions. Conventional text analysis often summarizes this information into numerical representations, which may not fully preserve the dynamic structure described in the narrative. In this study, we develop FinVision, a domain-specific multimodal generative model that translates financial narratives into visual representations of contemporaneous market dynamics. Using 87,940 paired observations of Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A) disclosures and stock price charts, FinVision learns the average relationship between a firm's narrative account of a fiscal year and its stock-price dynamics over the same period. Given an MD&A alone, the trained model generates a text-implied stock chart, extending financial text analysis beyond numerical representations toward text-to-visual generation. We validate FinVision in two ways. First, held-out tests show that the model generates stock-chart representations that closely resemble observed charts and outperform direct LLM prompting, general-purpose text-to-image models, and the same architecture without financial-domain adaptation. Second, we examine the economic validity of the generated representations by comparing text-implied and observed stock charts. The resulting discrepancy is systematically associated with weaker investor responses around subsequent 10-K filings and with worse subsequent market and operating outcomes. Overall, our findings show how domain-adapted multimodal generative AI can create a new visual representation of financial text and provide an additional modality for studying business communication.

TLDR: FinVision is a domain-specific multimodal model that maps textual financial MD&A disclosures to visual stock price charts, demonstrating superior performance over general-purpose generative models and offering a new way to analyze financial narratives.

TLDR: FinVision 是一个特定于金融领域的模态生成模型，能够将财务叙述性文本（MD&A）转换为对应的股价走势图，在金融文本可视化方面表现优于通用生成模型，并为商业沟通分析提供了新的视角。

PanDWM: Panoramic 4D Driving World Models

Driving world models usually generate videos from six pinhole cameras with separate optical centres and small overlaps, and must learn to keep these views consistent. We present PanDWM, which stitches them into one $360^\circ$ panoramic band. Camera boundaries then fall inside one image, so keeping the views consistent means keeping the image continuous, and each pixel still records its camera ray. Conditioned on layouts, a causal student generates the band window by window in four steps; it is distilled from a causal teacher and trained on its own rollouts. A feed-forward decoder lifts each frame along these rays into 3D Gaussians, and static Gaussians from neighbouring frames complete them into 4D scenes. On nuScenes, scored in one stitched $360^\circ$ view, PanDWM has lower FID and FVD than the multi-camera baselines at $8$-$46\times$ their speed, and keeps its 50-step teacher's FID while lowering FVD by 22-25%.

TLDR: PanDWM introduces a panoramic 360° representation for driving world models, simplifying cross-camera consistency into image continuity and utilizing a distillation-based generation process with 3D Gaussian Splatting for high-efficiency 4D scene synthesis.

TLDR: PanDWM 提出了一种用于自动驾驶世界模型的 360° 全景表示方法，通过将多相机一致性问题转化为图像连续性问题，并结合蒸馏生成与 3D 高斯泼溅技术，显著提升了 4D 场景生成的效率与质量。

Beyond Vehicle-Centric Data: Scaling Autonomous Driving through Roadside-to-Vehicle Generation

Training autonomous driving models requires large and diverse datasets, collected primarily by vehicles. Scaling them entails investment in fleet operation, data processing and annotation. Road infrastructure records traffic and multi-vehicle interactions, yet the viewpoint gap between roadside and onboard sensors limits its direct use for training. Here we introduce AnyR2V, a framework that transforms multimodal roadside observations into seven-camera surround-view videos for different vehicle perspectives. It aggregates sequential observations into a shared scene representation of geometry and road-user motion, then renders appearance, depth and layout conditions along target-vehicle trajectories. Supervised by spatiotemporally aligned roadside-vehicle pairs, AnyR2V generates surround-view videos guided by scene structure and road-user motion. Existing roadside 3D bounding boxes are transformed into target-vehicle coordinates and paired with generated images, allowing one traffic recording and its annotations to yield training data from multiple vehicle perspectives. We construct R2V360, a dataset of 89 real traffic sequences at an urban intersection, with aligned multimodal roadside data and onboard surround-view videos. AnyR2V outperforms six scene reconstruction methods in image fidelity and in depth and semantic agreement between generated and real onboard images. We further evaluate generated videos along other vehicles' trajectories by adding them to real training data. Adding generated frames equal to 50% of the original real training frame count improves mean average precision (mAP) by 3.3-3.4 AP-points across three 3D object detectors on the real onboard test set, relative to real-data-only baselines. These results demonstrate that roadside traffic records can provide training data from multiple vehicle perspectives, offering a new data source for autonomous driving.

TLDR: AnyR2V is a novel framework that leverages roadside infrastructure data to generate synthetic, multi-view training data for autonomous vehicles, significantly boosting 3D object detection performance.

TLDR: AnyR2V 框架通过将路侧传感器数据转化为多视角车载环视视频，为自动驾驶提供了一种高效的训练数据生成方案，显著提升了目标检测的精度。

A Threshold-Free Visual-Semantic Matching Metric for Evaluating Consistency in Subject-Driven Image Generation

In subject-driven text-to-image generation, quantitatively evaluating localized visual consistency across varying poses and environments remains a major challenge. Existing metrics either rely on global image embeddings (CLIP, DINO) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) that pool spatial representations and miss fine-grained textural artifacts, or depend on scalar threshold-gated dense metrics such as Visual-Semantic Matching (VSM) that suffer from point-alignment dropouts, severe threshold sensitivity, and score instability. To overcome these limitations, we propose TF-VSM, a fully continuous, threshold-free visual-consistency metric paired with an extended feature-disentanglement retraining schedule for dual-branch ResNet feature-aggregation networks. Rather than enforcing rigid binary cutoffs $(T_s,T_v)$, TF-VSM weights every object point by a non-linear semantic match confidence $w_j=ReLU(\hat{s}_j)^2$ and continuously integrates visual similarity over all consistency levels $r\in[0,1]$, yielding a closed-form confidence-weighted score without any hand-tuned hyperparameters. On a controlled 100-pair benchmark with ground-truth inpainting oracles, TF-VSM attains Pearson $0.638$ and Spearman $0.674$ oracle correlation, improving over the pretrained VSM baseline ($0.454/0.589$) by $+0.171/+0.116$ after retraining, and drastically outperforming CLIP ($-0.052/-0.004$), DINO ($0.087/0.121$), and VLMs ($0.096/0.003$). Six diagnostic ablations confirm that smooth confidence weighting is the right mechanism, that hard semantic gating is brittle (dropping to $0.533$ Pearson at $T_s{=}0.8$), and that the threshold-free metric is essentially invariant to the training-time loss balance $α$ that the thresholded metric still depends on.

TLDR: The paper introduces TF-VSM, a threshold-free metric for evaluating subject-driven image generation that replaces brittle hard-gating with continuous confidence weighting to improve fine-grained consistency measurement.

TLDR: 本文提出了一种名为 TF-VSM 的无阈值视觉一致性评估指标，通过连续置信度加权取代了传统方法中脆弱的硬阈值门控，显著提升了主体驱动图像生成任务中细粒度一致性评估的准确性和稳定性。

HEmIF-Bench: Benchmarking H&E-to-mIF Models BeyondVirtual-StainFidelity

Hematoxylin and eosin (H&E)-to-multiplex immunofluorescence (mIF) generators predict spatial protein expression from routine histology, yet pixel-level marker reconstruction does not establish downstream utility. We present HEmIF-Bench, evaluating five frozen generators across eight datasets spanning molecular, cellular, tissue, and patient scales. HEmIF-Bench decouples internal encoder features from generated outputs, benchmarking both against the pathology foundation reference UNI2. Mirroring the clinical workflow where pathologists rely on mIF to define cell phenotypes, delineate tertiary lymphoid structures (TLSs) and germinal centers (GCs), and stratify patient risk, we evaluate external CD3 recovery, single-cell classification, dense nuclear segmentation, TLS/GC segmentation, and patient survival prediction. Across the benchmark, foundation-model-based MIPHEI-ViT leads among the generators, while non-foundation models show task-specific strengths. Comparing the two routes reveals that internal encoder features generally outperform generated outputs, and larger output marker panels do not guarantee stronger downstream utility across datasets. Moreover, neither internal encoder features nor generated outputs consistently surpass frozen UNI2. These findings show that pixel-level stain fidelity does not equate to downstream utility, and that generators trained at current paired-data scales have yet to surpass large-scale pure-H&E foundation models. With the expansion of paired translation cohorts and the advancement of foundation-based generative architectures, HEmIF-Bench establishes a definitive benchmark for tracking when H&E-to-mIF generation crosses the threshold to deliver genuine value beyond routine histology for tumor microenvironment analysis and precision oncology.

TLDR: HEmIF-Bench evaluates H&E-to-mIF translation models by moving beyond pixel-level fidelity to assess clinical downstream utility, finding that current generators are generally outperformed by large-scale pathology foundation models.

TLDR: HEmIF-Bench通过评估H&E转mIF生成模型的临床下游效用（而非仅仅关注像素保真度），揭示了当前的生成模型尚未超越大规模病理基础模型。

Spectral Steering: Local Inverse Geometry for Frozen Diffusion Generators

A frozen diffusion generator may need to change several output properties while preserving others. We study when guidance faithfully realizes such a structured request and whether local inverse correction improves its direction. Our account links this benefit to the interaction between the request and the objective-specific remaining-sampler Jacobian: conditioning alone is insufficient. Spectral steering combines a request-specific cosine-gain predictor with damped inverse correction and fixed-time relinearization for finite edits. Scalar nulls and matched-movement controls test the predicted mechanism. Across 8,923 instances in a retrospectively identified local regime, predicted and realized gains correlate at Spearman 0.79, versus -0.11 for condition number. Experiments span images, audio, robotics, and control and planning, including complex character motion generation. On 50 paired inpainting cases, four corrections improve unobserved-pixel PSNR over Adam by 3.25 dB at equal total derivative traversals. Together, the analysis and controls connect request-dependent geometry to directional gains and establish where these gains translate into downstream performance.

TLDR: The paper introduces 'Spectral Steering,' a method for fine-grained control of frozen diffusion models by utilizing local inverse geometry to correct guidance directions, significantly improving edit fidelity across multiple modalities.

TLDR: 本文提出了“频谱导向”（Spectral Steering），通过利用局部逆几何结构来修正冻结扩散模型的引导方向，从而实现了在多种模态下对生成结果进行精准、结构化的精细编辑。

Consistency-Aware Map Generation at Multiple Zoom Levels Using Aerial Image

The multi-level tiled map service is widely used and serves as a kind of digital infrastructure. These map tiles are usually rendered from vector data, whose update needs to walk or drive with professional equipment to check every points of interest. This leads to inconvenience and expensive cost in timely updating maps. Compared with vector data, aerial images are much easier and cheaper to obtain. In this paper, we propose a novel multi-level map generation framework that can automatically generate accurate and consistent maps with multiple zoom levels from aerial images. It consists of a level-aware map generator and a consistency-aware map generator. The level-aware map generator is able to generate accurate initial maps with realistic details for each zoom level. The consistency-aware map generator regards initial maps at each zoom level as a sequence and builds the connection between them, so as to guarantee content consistency between maps at different zoom levels. Furthermore, we collect a large-scale high-quality dataset called Multi-Level Map (MLM) for map generation at multiple zoom levels. Experiments on our MLM dataset show that our method outperforms previous state-of-the-art map generation methods on both comprehensive quantitative metrics and perceptual quality.

TLDR: This paper proposes a framework for generating consistent, multi-level tiled maps from aerial imagery using a dual-generator architecture and introduces a new large-scale dataset (MLM) to address the limitations of manual vector map updates.

TLDR: 本文提出了一种基于航空影像生成多尺度一致性地图的框架，通过双生成器架构解决地图更新成本高的问题，并构建了一个大规模的多尺度地图（MLM）数据集。
