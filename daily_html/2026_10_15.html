Representation Conditioning for Generation

Representation-alignment methods effectively accelerate the diffusion transformer training. REPA, for example, uses a pretrained vision encoder to supervise an intermediate layer through an MLP projection head which is discarded after training. It aims to import semantics from the vision encoder to the diffusion backbone. While this objective is achieved at early layers by back-propagating the alignment loss, there is no guarantee that later layers, supervised by the velocity prediction loss alone, can well access such semantics. We propose a simple solution, representation conditioning (RECON), to improve semantics in later layers. During training, we feed the pretrained vision encoder tokens into later layers; during inference, because we no longer have the clean image, we reuse the MLP head (as a proxy to the vision encoder) and project its output tokens to the later layers. Our method adds no parameters and leads to much faster convergence: RECON is $63\times$ faster than SiT and $25\times$ faster than REPA. Code is available at https://github.com/saeidrazavi/RECON

TLDR: RECON introduces representation conditioning to improve semantic alignment in later diffusion transformer layers by feeding pretrained vision tokens during training and using an MLP proxy during inference, resulting in significant convergence speedups.

TLDR: RECON提出了一种表征调节方法，通过在训练中引入预训练视觉编码器标记并在推理中使用MLP代理，显著改善了扩散模型后续层的语义对齐，大幅提升了收敛速度。

CommerceGen-Studio: An End-to-End Framework for Commercial-Grade Main-Image Generation

We present CommerceGen-Studio, an end-to-end framework for generating commercial-grade main images for e-commerce Stock Keeping Units (SKUs). Our framework is organized along a three-stage pipeline of information extraction, semantic rewriting, and multi-condition generation: Prompt-Eval-Refine-Loop Agent (PERL-Agent) distills heterogeneous SKU metadata into concise selling-point text lists through an iterative closed loop of VLM and LLM roles scored by a reference-free rubric. Layout Thinker Optimization (LTO) aligns the rewriter with both caption and rendered-image quality via alternating domain-specific policy optimization. SKU-DiT, trained under Reinforce-Distill-Reinforce (RDR) progressive training, conditions jointly on the white-background product image, the trademark crop, and the structured layout to hold subject fidelity, trademark integrity, and character-level text accuracy together. The framework runs in production at a scale of millions of main images per day with a latency of a few seconds per image, which we believe makes it among the first systems of this kind to operate at commercial scale. We also release CommerceGen-Bench, a benchmark of hard cases collected from live traffic across all product categories. On it, our framework achieves the highest overall scores among the evaluated open-source baselines on both editing tracks, with and without prompt enhancement. It also matches much larger closed-source models, and beats several of them at Instruction Following and Product Integration - the two properties that determine whether an image can be published.

TLDR: CommerceGen-Studio is a scalable, end-to-end framework for e-commerce image generation, utilizing a multi-stage pipeline of VLM/LLM-based metadata refinement and a specialized DiT architecture to achieve high-fidelity commercial image production.

TLDR: CommerceGen-Studio 是一个用于电商商品主图生成的端到端框架，通过 VLM/LLM 元数据精炼与专门的 DiT 架构，实现了大规模、高保真度的商业级图像生成。

Any Time, Any Space: A World-State-Centric Framework for Agentic Video Generation

Long-form agentic video generation must turn a screenplay into shots that remain spatially and temporally consistent across nonconsecutive requests. Passing prompts or recent frames between shots does not provide a shared spatial frame for revisiting a scene or a time-indexed record for replaying an earlier event. We present Any Time, Any Space (ATAS), which compiles a screenplay into shot requests with explicit location, camera, and event-time references. Before generation, it retrieves relevant evidence from a scene memory of registered static geometry and an event memory of dynamic observations indexed by scene, event, and time. After generation, ATAS reconstructs and registers the clip. It updates memory only when review and state checks accept the observation, while film delivery remains independent of memory admission. Across four video generators, ATAS improves all reported cross-shot consistency measures and achieves the highest aggregate benchmark score for each backbone. It also outperforms the compared agents and 3D-guided methods on target-image spatial revisit, leads three event-recapture criteria, and receives the highest human ratings on all six criteria. The video results and code are available on our project page: https://lhmd.top/atas/.

TLDR: ATAS is a world-state-centric framework that uses structured scene and event memory to ensure spatial and temporal consistency in long-form agentic video generation.

TLDR: ATAS 是一个以世界状态为中心的框架，通过构建场景和事件记忆，解决了长视频生成中跨镜头空间和时间一致性的难题。

Speculative Sampling in Interactive Video Generation

Interactive video generation commonly relies on small bidirectional chunks to reduce response latency. Yet our preliminary experiments show that larger chunks improve video quality and better utilize GPU parallelism, allowing more latents to be sampled with only a modest increase in sampling time. Motivated by these observations, we propose Speculative Sampling, a strategy that combines the modeling power of large bidirectional windows with the low-latency interaction of small ones. It expands the modeling window by appending noise latents as padding and caches the resulting predictions for reuse when the condition remains unchanged. Predictions are delivered incrementally, while the uncommitted future remains replaceable. Our latency analysis shows that this removes the waiting-time lower bound imposed by chunk boundaries, shifting the optimization focus from reducing chunk size to accelerating per-chunk sampling. Bidirectional window size thus becomes a decision variable for jointly optimizing visual quality and sampling time, rather than a necessary constraint on low-latency interaction. This opens a path to serving large bidirectional models in interactive streaming systems without complex streaming post-training.

TLDR: The paper introduces 'Speculative Sampling' for interactive video generation, which uses padding and prediction caching to decouple bidirectional window size from latency, allowing for high-quality, real-time video generation.

TLDR: 本文提出了交互式视频生成的“推测采样”（Speculative Sampling）策略，通过填充噪声潜变量和缓存预测结果，打破了双向窗口大小与生成延迟之间的限制，从而在不增加复杂后训练的情况下实现高质量的实时视频流生成。

Generative Representation Learning for Scalable Latent Tokenization

We introduce an approach for self-supervised representation learning designed to be used as a tokenizer for generative modeling. Our objective supervises a representation loss by factorizing the pointwise mutual information of the data, which we estimate by taking information differences between conditional predictions from a learned generative model. We justify our algorithm theoretically and empirically, demonstrating that our generative approach to representation learning avoids dimensional collapse and feature suppression in cases where commonly used methods like DINO and LeJEPA fail. Moreover, we show our representation learning technique is scalable; expending more compute leads to predictably better latent tokenization. For the first time, we perform a careful analysis of the combined flop efficiency of tokenizer and generative model and demonstrate that our approach is a compute-optimal improvement over direct diffusion for pixel-space generation, obtaining an FID-50K of 2.18 with JiT-L. Applied to ImageNet and the text-to-image dataset GPIC, we show tokenizer encoder scaling laws for diffusability, where extrapolating our approach achieves representations in 7x fewer FLOPs than DINOv2 on ImageNet and 8x fewer FLOPs than WebSSL on GPIC.

TLDR: The paper introduces a self-supervised generative tokenizer that uses pointwise mutual information factorization to avoid dimensional collapse, achieving state-of-the-art compute-optimal scaling for diffusion-based image generation.

TLDR: 本文提出了一种基于生成式表示学习的Tokenization方法，通过因子化点互信息来避免维度坍塌，实现了在图像生成任务中具有计算最优性的扩展性表现。

PAWA: Panoramic Action-conditioned World Modeling for Multiple Agents

A multiplayer world model must predict how the independent actions of several players change a shared environment, including changes outside a player's current field of view. A point-of-view (POV) video captures such changes only after they enter its field of view, whereas panoramic video records the surroundings in every viewing direction and has served as an intermediate representation of the world state in recent world models. Learning this task therefore requires synchronized POV and panoramic videos of interacting players together with their actions, yet no existing dataset provides this combination in Minecraft, an established testbed in which players act independently in a shared, modifiable 3D world. We present PAWA, a dataset and framework for action-conditioned panoramic multiplayer world modeling. The dataset pairs each player's POV video with world-stabilized panoramic video and aligned actions. Its distributed pipeline decouples CPU gameplay capture from GPU rendering, scales beyond 64 simultaneous players, and re-renders each recorded session under new observation configurations without replaying the gameplay. On this dataset, we develop a dual-tower model that jointly predicts POV and panoramic video and couples the two streams through cross-attention that aligns tokens observing the same direction in a shared world frame. We further introduce a quadratic-phase player encoding that provides many more player codes than existing rotary codes at the same embedding dimension. Experiments show that PAWA outperforms state-of-the-art multiplayer world models in POV prediction quality while also producing high-fidelity panoramic predictions. The dataset and collection pipeline are open source to support further research on multiplayer world models.

TLDR: PAWA is a novel framework and dataset for multiplayer world modeling in Minecraft, utilizing a dual-tower model to predict synchronized POV and panoramic video via cross-attention.

TLDR: PAWA 是一个用于 Minecraft 多人世界建模的框架和数据集，它通过双塔模型和交叉注意力机制，实现了 POV 视频与全景视频的联合预测。

Glance Forcing: Making Bidirectional Diffusion Autoregressive at a Glance

Autoregressive generation underlies real-time, causal video synthesis and is the backbone of video world models. Today's dominant approach distills a bidirectional diffusion model into a causal few-step student, but existing pipelines make this conversion prohibitively expensive, requiring offline Probability Flow ODE} (PF-ODE) trajectories that cost tens of thousands of GPU-hours and terabytes of storage. We show it can be made dramatically more efficient. Pushing this efficiency to its extreme, we reduce trajectory supervision to a single sample, the strictest setting possible, and find that it causes severe quality collapse. We trace this to the teacher's trajectory geometry: the high-noise regime, governing semantic layout and motion, and the low-noise regime, governing fine texture exhibit fundamentally different curvature and optimization behavior, so a single monolithic student cannot jointly fit both, causing severe representation interference. Motivated by this, we propose Glance Forcing, a lightweight distillation framework that decouples the denoising trajectory into two complementary slow and fast experts, each guided by a tailored, stage-specific objective, while keeping the pretrained teacher entirely frozen. This decoupling further reveals a general mechanism underlying few-step distillation: PF-ODE distillation mainly straightens the few-step trajectory geometry, while the subsequent distribution-matching stage independently governs distributional quality. Using only one PF-ODE trajectory, Glance Forcing already surpasses the fully-trained state-of-the-art baseline, raising the VBench Total score from 84.04 to 84.22 while cutting trajectory-collection cost from thousands of GPU-hours to about one A100-GPU-hour, with markedly larger gains in dynamic degree, VisionReward, and instruction-following, and consistent preference from human raters over strong baselines.

TLDR: Glance Forcing is a highly efficient distillation framework that enables causal few-step video generation by decoupling denoising into slow and fast experts, significantly reducing training costs while improving performance.

TLDR: Glance Forcing 提出了一种高效的蒸馏框架，通过将去噪轨迹解耦为慢速和快速专家，在极低训练成本下实现了高质量的因果视频生成。

StreamErase: Towards Ultra-Fast Streaming Video Erasing

Current video object removal methods suffer from prohibitive latency (minutes per video) and non-causal processing that prevents real-time interaction. We present StreamErase, enabling sub-second latency with up to 26 FPS streaming generation on a single GPU. Our approach first augments a text-to-video model with mask input to generate high-quality videos that erase target objects and synthesize coherent backgrounds, but does not perform inference on the fly. As such, we progressively transfer the erasing capability from a powerful bidirectional foundation model to an efficient unidirectional streaming eraser, enabling long-horizon erasing without sacrificing visual fidelity. Several key challenges arise when generating videos over long, potentially infinite horizons: (1) bridging the domain gap from training on finite length and extrapolating to long horizons, (2) sustaining high quality by preventing error accumulation, and (3) maintaining fast inference, without increasing computational cost as the context grows. To address these challenges, we carefully designed sliding-window causal attention with attention sinks. By incorporating self-rollout with attention sinks and KV cache rolling during training, we simulate inference-time extrapolations with a fixed context window, enabling constant-speed generation of arbitrarily long videos. Finally, we establish a dedicated benchmark, LongErase, for streaming long-video object removal. Our model achieves strong performance compared to existing video object erasing models and generalizes well to real-world video scenarios, uniquely enabling infinite-length streaming. With StreamErase, users can input a mask and see the erasing results unfold in real-time, delivering a truly interactive experience.

TLDR: StreamErase introduces a real-time, causal video object removal framework that enables 26 FPS inference by distilling a bidirectional foundation model into a unidirectional streaming model using sliding-window attention and attention sinks.

TLDR: StreamErase 提出了一种实时视频物体擦除框架，通过将双向基础模型蒸馏为使用滑动窗口注意力与注意力槽（Attention Sinks）的单向流式模型，实现了 26 FPS 的实时视频擦除效果。

RepForcing: Representation-Space Initialization for Autoregressive Video Distillation

Distilling pretrained video diffusion models into few-step autoregressive (AR) generators typically combines student initialization with distribution matching distillation (DMD). An intermediate AR teacher can provide causally compatible targets for ODE initialization. However, causally compatible targets alone do not ensure high-quality generation under a short sampling schedule. We analyze the posterior-mean predictions favored by direct latent regression and empirically find that causal ODE initialization alleviates blur but still leaves fine detail unresolved. To address this limitation, we propose Representation Forcing, which learns few-step causal initialization through representation-space supervision rather than intermediate-teacher trajectory regression. The student is trained with teacher forcing by comparing clean predictions and training targets through normalized features of a frozen video diffusion transformer. This phase reuses the synthetic videos generated by a bidirectional model for AR teacher training and replaces both teacher training and causal ODE distillation, without generating additional causal ODE trajectories. The subsequent asymmetric DMD procedure remains unchanged. Empirical results show that, at four sampling steps, RepForcing better preserves generation quality than intermediate-teacher-based initialization before DMD. After DMD, our four-step chunk-wise model improves VBench Total from 84.04 to 84.66 (+0.62 points) and VisionReward from 6.326 to 10.074 (+59.25%) over the reported causal-ODE-based baseline.

TLDR: RepForcing improves few-step autoregressive video distillation by using representation-space supervision instead of trajectory regression, significantly boosting visual quality and reward metrics in four-step generation.

TLDR: RepForcing 通过引入表征空间监督而非轨迹回归来改进自回归视频蒸馏，显著提升了四步视频生成中的图像质量与奖励指标。

When Pairwise Preferences Cycle: A Reliability-Anchored Preference Walk for Video GRPO

Pairwise preference feedback has become a widely adopted supervision signal for reinforcement learning in video generation models, as pairwise comparisons provide a more reliable alternative than pointwise scalar ratings for evaluating the quality of generated videos. Specifically, we define a preference graph for each GRPO group under a pairwise evaluator, where nodes represent sampled rollouts and edges encode pairwise preferences. Auditing 6,976 on-policy comparison graphs, we find that cyclic preferences are prevalent and persist throughout training. Naive pairwise reward aggregation based on average win rates discards the underlying comparison structures and treats each rollout independently, which may introduce unreliable advantage estimates for certain rollouts and consequently affect reinforcement learning optimization. Therefore, we propose the \emph{Reliability-Anchored Preference Walk} (\ours{}), a graph-aware advantage aggregation method that strengthens the global preference signal from win rates with reliable relational evidence from cyclic preference graphs through a reliability-guided preference walk. Under matched evaluator, comparison protocol, group size, prompt schedule, and query budget, \ours{} requires no additional evaluator queries beyond the observed comparisons within each rollout group and improves over Pairwise-GRPO across controlled comparisons, transfer settings, and blind human evaluations.

TLDR: The paper identifies cyclic preferences as a common issue in video generation RLHF and proposes 'Reliability-Anchored Preference Walk' (RAPW), a graph-aware advantage aggregation method that improves training stability and performance without additional evaluator queries.

TLDR: 本文指出视频生成强化学习中的成对偏好循环问题，并提出了“可靠性锚定偏好游走”（RAPW）方法，通过利用循环偏好图中的关系证据优化奖励聚合，在不增加额外评估开销的情况下提升了模型训练效果。

BluePixel: Toward Native 4K Agentic Image Editing

Instruction-driven image editing has reached native 4K, yet how such editors are trained remains undisclosed, and pretraining one from scratch is beyond most teams. The task itself compounds the difficulty: a single end-to-end editor cannot make precise parametric adjustments, match specialized experts, or interpret the vague instructions users actually write. We argue that 4K editing is a system problem rather than a single-model problem, and present BluePixel, which couples a native-4K editing model with an agent harness; the complete training recipe is disclosed in this paper. The model makes 4K training practical: it turns a 1K-pretrained editor into a native-4K one through progressive VAE adaptation that keeps 4K training as affordable as 1K, absorbs a long tail of vertical editing skills through continual learning, and refines the aesthetic-fidelity trade-off with reinforcement learning. Distillation via source-anchored target projection (SATP) then compresses 50 denoising steps to 4, completing a 4K edit in 8.7 s on a single Kunlunxin P800 XPU while preserving task success rate and background consistency. The harness, BluePixel Agent, makes editing controllable: it decomposes user instructions into specialist and general operations, verifies every result with the same reward model that guided training, and retries within bounded attempts. On VINS-4KEval++ (3,305 native-4K cases), BluePixel ranks second among seven models, behind only GPT Image 2 in non-edited-region consistency.

TLDR: BluePixel is an agentic framework for native 4K image editing that utilizes progressive VAE adaptation, reinforcement learning, and a task-decomposing agent harness to achieve efficient, high-fidelity edits.

TLDR: BluePixel 是一款用于原生 4K 图像编辑的代理式框架，通过渐进式 VAE 适配、强化学习以及任务分解代理，实现了高效且高保真的图像编辑效果。

PhyMetric: Diagnosing Physical Plausibility in Text-to-Video Generation via Scene-Level QA

Text-to-video (T2V) generation models can produce visually realistic videos that nevertheless violate the physical consequences implied by the prompt and scene. Existing benchmarks largely rely on holistic plausibility scores or broad quality dimensions, leaving it unclear which physical behavior is being tested and why a model fails. We introduce PhyMetric, a scene-level diagnostic benchmark that recasts physical plausibility evaluation as verifiable, scene-grounded question answering. PhyMetric contains 1{,}115 scenes spanning 50 physical categories across six domains, paired with 8{,}920 answer-balanced binary questions over 12 evaluation dimensions. Each scene is associated with eight targeted yes/no questions and a structured physical prior specifying the governing law, expected temporal sequence, observable signatures, and common failure modes. At evaluation time, a vision-language model (VLM) judge answers each question from question-aware sampled frames conditioned on the prior, and correctness is hierarchically aggregated into scene, dimension, physical category, and model scores. Experiments on four open-source T2V models and three VLM judges show that scene-specific binary QA is a critical component of the protocol: when each targeted question is replaced by a single generic plausibility query, evaluator accuracy drops to the chance level. Structured physical priors offer a complementary signal that improves human alignment of the resulting scores, with effects that vary across VLM judges of different capacity. These findings establish PhyMetric as a benchmark that turns physical plausibility evaluation from opaque holistic scoring into interpretable, scene-level inspection. Our project page is available at: https://mengsiwei.github.io/PhyMetric/.

TLDR: PhyMetric is a new diagnostic benchmark for text-to-video generation that replaces holistic plausibility scoring with structured, scene-grounded binary QA to better identify specific physical violations.

TLDR: PhyMetric 是一个针对文生视频模型的诊断基准，它通过结构化的场景问答机制，将模糊的整体合理性评估转化为可验证的具体物理行为检测。

StateCraft: Augmenting Video World Models with Abstract-State Reasoning

Video world models aim to simulate complex environments with realistic appearance and dynamics. Maintaining consistency over long interactions requires tracking past actions and understanding how those actions affect the environment. Video world models can only generate partial observations of the entire world at a time. Remembering past observations alone may not be sufficient in every scenario, as they may no longer reflect the world after a sequence of actions. We introduce a paradigm that decouples a persistent, updatable abstract state from the visual state. The abstract state keeps track of the entities' changes as the world evolves, and is used to condition the visual state, which represents the current view. We propose StateCraft, a novel architecture that pairs a language model with a video diffusion model to evolve the dynamic world. We evaluate our approach on generative interfaces, which pose a substantial challenge because a single action may change parts of the state that are not currently visible. To support training and evaluation, we develop a data collection pipeline that produces informative, densely annotated trajectories at scale without human or agent supervision, together with a benchmark measuring generation quality, memory, and controllability. On this benchmark, our approach improves surpasses the state-of-the-art baselines. We demonstrate that StateCraft maintains high self-consistency and strong generation quality over long trajectories. Because the abstract state is explicit, it can be edited directly, and generation follows edits to states never observed in the data. Finally, a proof-of-concept 3D experiment, in which actions change the illumination of an unobserved room, shows that the paradigm can also be applied to spatial environments. Project page: https://insait-institute.github.io/StateCraft/.

TLDR: StateCraft decouples abstract state tracking from visual generation, using a language model to maintain environment consistency in video world models, even when changes occur outside the current field of view.

Generative World Simulation with DAO and JING

We present a Generative World Simulation framework that couples persistent shared world state with continuous first-person audiovisual generation. The framework integrates DAO, a computable world engine, and JING, an Egocentric Interactive Experience Model, to support sustained interaction for humans and agents. DAO maintains the states of characters, objects, and spaces and their relationships, advancing the world according to interaction rules and world time while preserving the consequences of past actions. From this evolving state, DAO derives observer-specific conditions for JING, which combines these conditions with character, object, and scene references, observation history, and current actions to generate each observer's audiovisual experience. Users can explore environments, manipulate objects, and communicate with characters through viewpoint control and text instructions, with their actions shaping both the shared state and subsequent experiences. By connecting world-state evolution with experience generation in a continuous interaction loop, the framework offers an approach to building interactive worlds that retain their history, evolve through action, and persist beyond any individual observer.

TLDR: The paper introduces a framework that synchronizes a persistent, rule-based world engine (DAO) with an egocentric audiovisual generation model (JING) to create interactive, historical, and evolving simulated worlds.

SceneAE: Scene Generation and Reconstruction in Queryable 3D-Anchored Latent Space

Recent video world models have increasingly adopted 3D memory mechanisms to integrate historical observations into a lightweight and persistent scene representation. This naturally raises a complementary question: if 3D representations can serve as an effective mechanism for memorizing observed scenes, can they also be directly used as the latent space for reconstructing and generating scenes? Motivated by this idea, we represent a scene as a compact and unstructured 3D-anchored latent space (\textbf{SceneAE}). Each latent combines an explicit 3D position with an implicit content feature and is spatially grounded to describe its local scene region, forming a sparse latent point cloud. Unlike conventional video-VAE latents that describe how a scene appears from particular viewpoints, our representation directly describes the scene itself, making it naturally applicable to both reconstruction and generation. Into the LidarVerse: A Real-Time Unified LiDAR World Model Across Tasks and Sensors

We present LidarVerse, a real-time unified LiDAR world model for sensor simulation across tasks and sensors. Existing LiDAR simulators largely follow one of two complementary paradigms: reconstruction-based methods preserve the geometry of a captured scene but require lengthy per-scene optimization, whereas generative methods amortize learning across data but lack the consistency needed for long-horizon rollouts. LidarVerse brings both paradigms into a single, unified conditional generative framework. A universal tokenizer maps LiDAR range sequences into a shared latent space, where a bidirectional teacher learns a common interface for conditioning on a LiDAR prefix, an HD-Map, a persistent spatial cache, and optionally, text. Varying the available scene evidence turns the same model into a generator of new worlds, a point-cloud forecaster, or a generalizable resimulator of observed scenes without per-scene optimization. We distill the teacher into a four-step autoregressive causal student and maintain both a rolling temporal K/V cache and a world-aligned spatial cache, enabling persistent long-horizon rollouts with real-time inference at 11 FPS on a single H100 GPU. LidarVerse achieves state-of-the-art performance on most reported metrics across all three evaluation protocols, while its tokenizer supports 32-, 64-, and 128-beam LiDAR and generalizes to an unseen scanning pattern. These results demonstrate that generative and reconstruction-grounded LiDAR simulation can be realized within one model, providing the core capabilities for future closed-loop sensor simulation.

TLDR: LidarVerse is a unified generative world model that bridges reconstruction-based and generative LiDAR simulation, enabling real-time, long-horizon sensor forecasting through a universal tokenizer and dual-cache architecture.
